How to trainGuide

Daily loop

  • Puzzles — tactical shots verified by the built-in engine. Failed attempts reveal the solution.
  • Endgames — short mate patterns (KQK, KRK, KBBK, KNNK) with unique solutions.
  • Openings — replay repertoire lines, or take a quiz on the opening you are playing.
  • Analysis — load a PGN and get a full evaluation of every move.

Board controls

  • Click a piece, then a target square — or drag and drop.
  • Right-click a square to move it there (when legal).
  • ← → browse moves, U take back, F flip, H hint.

Notation

The move list uses standard algebraic notation: N knight, B bishop, R rook, Q queen, K king, x capture, = promotion, + check, # mate, O-O castling.

About

This trainer is a single self-contained HTML file: no network calls, no external libraries, no fonts, no images. The chess rules, SAN parser, puzzle generator and search engine are all written in plain JavaScript inside the file, so it works offline straight from your filesystem.
